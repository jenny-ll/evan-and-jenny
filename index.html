Dress Code

Welcome Party

Dressy casual
We invite guests to wear elevated casual wear such as collared shirts, blouses, trousers, skirts, or dresses. Think stylish yet comfortable.

Elegant, polished attire is encouraged! Think suits and ties, midi or maxi dresses, cocktail dresses, or jumpsuits. Vancouver evenings can be cool, so a wrap or light jacket is recommended.

Beautiful British Columbia

Where to Stay

We will not have designated hotel room blocks so guests can choose the location and style that works best for them. Below are a few areas and hotel options we recommend based on our local knowledge. Please book early, as Vancouver hotels can fill up quickly, especially in late spring.

Near Cecil Green Park House (UBC / Point Grey)

Best for staying close to the venue and enjoying a quieter area. Good options include hotels near UBC campus or Point Grey.

Downtown Vancouver

Best for guests who want easy access to restaurants, shopping, and sightseeing. Downtown offers the widest range of hotels and is about a 20 to 30 minute drive from the venue depending on traffic.

Kitsilano / West Broadway

A convenient middle-ground with boutique hotels and short-term stay options, plus easy access to the beach and a straightforward drive to Cecil Green Park House.
